1. Principles and audience

Every page is for Canadian small business owners: owner-operators who are short on time. They aren't investors, enterprise readers or a general business audience. Design for someone checking a CRA deadline between customers.

  • Useful first. Deadlines, grants, rates and tools take priority over decoration.
  • Editorial, not corporate. The site should feel like a magazine. Headlines and real photography do the work, not gradients or illustrations.
  • Calm and scannable. Strong headings, short excerpts and clear dates and reading times.
  • CanadianSME is the flagship. Sister titles (AmericanSMB, EuropeanSMB, Black Business, Startup Growth, She & Success, Indigenous-SME) are always visually secondary.
  • Canadian spelling everywhere: labour, centre, organisation (when the brand uses it), cheque.

2. Foundations

2.2 Typography

There are two families, both free on Google Fonts. Sora (600–800) is for headings, labels, buttons and numbers. IBM Plex Sans (400–600) is for body text, excerpts, forms and meta.

Headlines use sentence or title case, never all caps. Balance wrapping on headings and keep excerpts to about 2–3 lines. Minimum text size is 11px, used only for labels.

2.3 Layout and spacing

  • Content width: 1280px max on the main site and 1180px on Tools, with 24px side padding on all sizes.
  • Section padding: 64–72px top and bottom, and 26–40px for slim bands (This week, Trending topics, sponsor banners).
  • Grid: 12-column feel, built as auto-fit card grids (min 220–280px per card) so they wrap naturally. The Latest section is main column + 320px sidebar.
  • Spacing steps: 4, 8, 12, 16, 22, 28, 44, 64, 72px. Use gaps between items, not one-off margins.
  • Breakpoints: full navigation at 1240px and wider. Below that it switches to the Menu button and drawer. Cards stack to one column under 560px.
  • Sticky elements: the header is always sticky. Sidebars stick 96px from the top.

2.4 Shape, lines and elevation

  • Radius: 8px for buttons and inputs, 10px for cards and panels, 999px for pills and chips, 50% for avatars and play buttons, 4–6px for small tags.
  • Borders: 1px Line (#E4E7EC) on cards. Inputs use a 1.5px Line Strong border.
  • Shadow: almost none. The header has a 1px hairline shadow. Cards rely on borders, not shadows.
  • Focus: 3px Award Gold outline with a 2px offset on every interactive element.

5. Homepage, section by section

Sections in order. Alternate white and Mist backgrounds, and use dark Ink bands for Magazine, Woman of the Month and Podcast. Never stack two dark bands.

Hero, This week and Trending

One lead story: category pill, meta, display headline, excerpt, image on the right. Then four under-hero stories. The dark “This week” band shows the next CRA deadline with a live countdown, the GST/HST calculator, provincial funding and the newsletter. Trending topics is a slim Mist strip.

Latest Business Insights

Filter chips, a card grid of ten articles with “Load more”, and a sticky sidebar with Most Read. Reading time is calculated from real word counts.

Sponsored content and sponsor banners

Three partner cards, always labelled Sponsored. A Mist strip of sponsor logos below. These are placeholders until partner material is supplied.

Magazine

Dark band. The latest cover large, “Read the issue” and subscribe actions, and recent covers.

AI coverage and Topics

One lead and two supporting AI stories, then the topic directory.

Entrepreneur Spotlight

Mist band. A featured interview with photo, name, company and a pull quote, used only if the quote exists in the article.

Free tools and CRA deadlines

Calculator cards linking to the Tools page, then the upcoming deadline list.

Business Woman of the Month

Dark band with gold accents. Photo, name, role, company and a genuine quote from the article.

Communities

Four blocks (Women, Black, Indigenous, 2SLGBTQI+). Each has a lead and two secondary stories from its own dataset only. Never mix communities.

Province picker

Mist band. Pick a province to filter grants, rules and resources.

Podcast

Dark band. The latest episode with a red play button, and recent episodes.

Events, Resources and Partners

Event cards with dates and Register, resource guides, then partner logos.

6. Inner page templates

All inner pages share the header, a Mist breadcrumb bar, a Sora 800 H1 (50 → 32px) and the footer. Body content sits at a maximum width of 1280px. Long reading copy is narrower, about 720px.

Article

Category pill, H1, standfirst, author with avatar, published and updated dates, reading time, share buttons, then a 720px body. Sidebar: “In this article” contents, newsletter sign-up. Related articles at the end.

Category

H1 with the category name, filter chips, a card grid and pagination.

Search results

Large search field, result count, mixed results (articles, podcasts, resources, events).

Magazine archive

A grid of covers, newest first, with issue number and month.

Magazine issue

Cover, issue title, “Inside this issue” list, read and subscribe actions.

Podcast archive

Episode list with play buttons, guest, duration and date.

Podcast episode

Player, episode title, guest, episode notes.

Events listing

Upcoming events (Summit, Awards, Expo) with dates, location and Register.

Event detail

Event name, date, venue, “What to expect” and a primary Register button.

Business resources

Guides grouped by topic: starting, funding, tax, HR, exporting.

About

Mission, leadership, team, approach, and the events and titles Cmarketing runs. Sister titles are kept visually secondary.

Advertising

Display, sponsored content, magazine and event options. Media kit request form.

Contact

Contact form (name, email, topic, message) with inline validation.

Contributor

Author bio, photo and recent work.

Subscribe

A single-purpose sign-up page with one clear benefit.

404 page

Plain message with search and the main site links.

7. Tools (calculators)

A separate page with its own sticky header and calculator tabs, max width 1180px. Inputs on the left and results on the right. Every result shows the tax year and the sources of the rates. Warnings use the amber status colours. No sign-up is required.

8. Content, imagery and accessibility rules

  • Write for an owner-operator: concrete Canadian topics such as CRA deadlines, BDC and EDC financing, provincial grants, tariffs and Bank of Canada decisions.
  • Use only real featured images from the article. No stock, AI-generated or illustrated substitutes. Leave the slot empty until a photo arrives.
  • Never invent quotes, names or numbers. Pull quotes must exist in the source article.
  • Dates follow the format “Aug 18, 2026”. Reading time is rounded to whole minutes.
  • Body text contrast must be at least 4.5:1. Never use grey on red, or red body text on Ink.
  • Every image needs real alt text. Decorative icons get none.
  • Keyboard: every menu, chip and button is reachable and shows the gold focus ring.
  • Mobile hit targets are at least 44px, and body text is at least 15px on mobile.

9. Open items before launch

  • Links for the Black Entrepreneurship articles (they currently point to the #black anchor).
  • Partner logos and sponsored content creative.
  • Photos for the sample articles in Latest Business Insights (sample copy is still to be replaced with real articles).
  • Current Bank of Canada policy rate, if it is to appear in the This week band.
