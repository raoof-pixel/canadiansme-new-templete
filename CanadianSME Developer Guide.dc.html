1. Project overview and deliverables

The site is an editorial magazine for Canadian small business owners. Every decision serves an owner-operator who is short on time: CRA deadlines, federal and provincial funding, BDC/EDC financing, tariffs and provincial rules come before decoration. CanadianSME is the flagship. Sister titles (AmericanSMB, EuropeanSMB, Black Business, Startup Growth, She & Success, Indigenous-SME) must never outrank it visually. Use Canadian spelling throughout (labour, centre, cheque).

2. Architecture and stack

The prototype is a single-page app that switches pages with #hash routes. Do not ship it that way. Search engines ignore everything after #, so every page must be a real URL that returns server-rendered HTML.

LayerRecommendation
CMSWordPress is being retired at handover. Use a headless CMS (Sanity, Payload or Strapi) as the editors’ admin. Migrate posts, categories, authors and media into it (section 15). Content types: province, event, resource, offer, issue, episode, sponsored, news_item (fields in section 8).
Front endNext.js or Astro, using static generation plus incremental revalidation (rebuild a page when the CMS publishes). Every page must be server-rendered.
Scheduled jobsVercel Cron or Netlify Scheduled Functions for the news feed (section 16) and nightly hiding of expired offers and past deadlines.
StylingThe CSS custom properties in section 4. The prototype uses inline styles for speed of iteration. Production should use a stylesheet or utility classes built from the same tokens.
SearchThe prototype searches articles, provinces, policies and pages on the client. Production: Algolia or Meilisearch for typo tolerance. Results page at /search/?q=, set to noindex.
HostingVercel or Netlify, with a CDN in front of everything. Serve images through a resizing service (WebP/AVIF). Use HTTPS with HSTS.
FontsSora (600, 700, 800) and IBM Plex Sans (400, 500, 600). Self-host as WOFF2 with font-display:swap, and preload Sora 600 and Plex 400.

9. SEO specification

The prototype already sets all of the following per page (see applySeo() in the homepage file). Production must output the same tags in the server HTML, not only through JavaScript. Generate them in the framework’s metadata layer from CMS fields.

9.1 Tags on every page

TagRule

<title>{Page title} | CanadianSME, at most about 60 characters before the brand. Homepage: “CanadianSME Small Business Magazine | Canadian Business Insights”.
meta descriptionUnique per page, 150–158 characters. Articles use the excerpt, provinces the intro, categories a templated line.
canonicalAbsolute, https, trailing slash, no tracking parameters. Paginated archives point to themselves (do not canonicalise page 2 to page 1).
robotsindex, follow, max-image-preview:large. Search results and 404s: noindex, follow. Staging: noindex on everything plus HTTP basic auth.

HeadingsExactly one H1 per page. The homepage has a visually hidden H1 because the slider headlines rotate. Keep heading levels in order: H2 for sections, H3 for cards.
ImagesDescriptive alt text on every content image (empty alt="" on decorative ones), explicit width/height, loading="lazy" below the fold, and WebP/AVIF output.

9.3 Crawling and indexing

  • /sitemap.xml: a sitemap index, split into posts, pages, provinces, events and categories, with real lastmod dates. A Google News sitemap covers posts from the last 48 hours. The starter file is seo/sitemap.xml.
  • /robots.txt: starter in seo/robots.txt (blocks /search and /wp-admin/, and declares the sitemap).
  • Submit to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. Verify both the www and apex domains, and redirect one to the other with a 301.
  • An RSS feed at /feed/, linked in the head.
  • Redirects: map every current canadiansme.ca URL that changes to its new URL with a 301. Crawl the live site before launch and keep the redirect map in the repo.
  • Internal linking: related articles, breadcrumbs, category and province links on every article. No orphan pages, and the HTML sitemap at /sitemap/ lists everything.
  • Outbound links to partners and sponsors: rel="sponsored". User-submitted links: rel="ugc". Government sources (CRA, BDC, EDC, canada.ca) stay followed.

9.4 Content SEO for editors

  • Target queries Canadian owners actually search: “GST/HST filing deadline”, “Ontario small business grants”, “BDC loan”, “how to register a business in Alberta”. Put the province or programme name in the title and first paragraph.
  • Show updated dates on time-sensitive pages (deadlines, rates, grants) and actually refresh them.
  • Province pages and tools are evergreen ranking assets. Link to them from every relevant article.

11. Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA, AODA)

  • Ontario’s AODA requires WCAG 2.0 AA. Target 2.2 AA.
  • A skip link to #main, landmark elements (header, nav, main, footer), and one H1 per page.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1. Never grey text on red, and never red body text on ink.
  • Every control is keyboard-reachable and shows the gold focus ring. Menus, drawer and modals close on Escape, and modals trap and restore focus.
  • The carousel has pause controls and doesn’t auto-advance under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Forms have visible labels, errors announced through aria-live, and 44px targets.

12. Performance

  • Core Web Vitals targets at the 75th percentile on mobile: LCP under 2.5s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.1.
  • Render only the current page. The prototype originally mounted all 22 page templates at once, which caused lag. That is now fixed and must stay that way in production.
  • The hero image or first slide is the LCP element. Preload it, and don’t lazy-load it. Everything below the fold gets loading="lazy".
  • Responsive images through srcset/sizes, with width and height always set to prevent layout shift.
  • Defer non-critical JavaScript (slider, pop-up, search suggestions). Load analytics after consent.
  • Cache HTML at the CDN and purge on publish. Static assets are immutable and fingerprinted.

13. QA and launch checklist

  • ☐ Every route in section 3 returns 200 with server-rendered HTML. Unknown URLs return 404.
  • ☐ Unique title, description and canonical on every page (crawl with Screaming Frog).

  • ☐ Sitemap submitted, robots.txt live, staging noindex removed.
  • ☐ 301 redirect map tested against the old site crawl.
  • ☐ Lighthouse mobile: Performance ≥ 90, Accessibility ≥ 95, SEO 100.

15. Leaving WordPress: migration checklist

The live site runs on WordPress. The new build does not. Losing URLs loses Google rankings, so treat redirects as launch-blocking.

  • ☐ Crawl the live canadiansme.ca (Screaming Frog) and export every indexed URL before anything changes.
  • ☐ Export all posts, pages, categories, tags, authors and media (WP REST API or WXR export). Import into the CMS, preserving slugs, publish dates and authors.
  • ☐ Copy every image from /wp-content/uploads/ to the new image host. Rewrite image URLs in article bodies.
  • ☐ Clean the body HTML: strip shortcodes, page-builder markup and inline styles.
  • ☐ Build the 301 redirect map: every old URL → its new URL (including /category/, /tag/, /author/, /page/2/ and ?p=123 forms). Never redirect everything to the homepage.
  • ☐ Replace each plugin: SEO (metadata layer), forms (email platform + CRM, section 10), ads and sponsor slots, newsletter pop-up, caching (CDN).
  • ☐ Keep RSS: /feed/ must still work (redirect or regenerate) for existing subscribers.
  • ☐ After launch: resubmit the sitemap in Google Search Console, watch the Coverage and 404 reports daily for two weeks, keep the old WordPress read-only for 30 days, then shut it down.

16. Canada SME News: automated feed (deferred, not in launch scope)

On hold. The News page and homepage strip have been removed from the prototype. Do not build this section, or add /news/ to the sitemap, until the client confirms an editor to own it. The spec below is kept for later.

Short, dated news items for owners: rate decisions, CRA changes, grant intakes, tariffs and provincial rules. Shown on /news/ (prototype #news), the homepage “Canada SME News” strip (5 latest), matching province pages and the Tuesday newsletter. AI drafts, a human approves. Nothing auto-publishes.

16.1 Pipeline

StepWhat happens
1. FetchScheduled job every 60 minutes. Pull the RSS/Atom feeds and newsroom pages listed in 16.2. Skip any item whose source URL is already stored.
2. FilterSend title + first 2,000 characters to the Claude API. It returns relevant: true/false plus a reason. Keep only items that change what a Canadian owner-operator must do, pay or can apply for.
3. SummariseSecond call returns JSON: headline, summary, topic, provinces[], deadline (ISO or null), confidence. Validate against a schema; reject on failure.
4. DraftSave as a news_item with status draft. Notify the editor (email or Slack) with a daily digest.
5. ReviewEditor checks every number and date against the source, edits if needed, approves or rejects. Target: 10 minutes a day.
6. PublishOn approve, the CMS webhook revalidates /news/, the homepage and affected province pages.

16.2 Sources (official only)

Bank of Canada, Canada Revenue Agency, Department of Finance Canada, ISED, Statistics Canada (The Daily), CBSA, Global Affairs Canada (trade), BDC, EDC, and the 13 provincial and territorial government newsrooms. Do not ingest other publishers’ news sites. Confirm each feed URL at build time and store them in a CMS-editable list.

16.3 AI instructions (system prompt outline)

  • Audience: a Canadian small business owner who is short on time. Plain language, Canadian spelling, no hype.
  • Use only facts in the supplied source text. Never infer amounts, rates or dates. If unsure, set confidence: low.
  • Headline states the change, not the announcement (“CRA extends T2 filing deadline to …”, not “CRA announces update”).
  • Summary sentence 2 always answers “what this means for your business”.
  • Never copy more than one short phrase from the source.

16.4 Rules

  • Every item shows source name, source date and a link to the original. Summaries only, never full text.
  • confidence: low items are flagged in the review queue and can’t be bulk-approved.
  • Items with a deadline also appear in the This week strip until the date passes.
  • Disclosure line on /news/: items are summarised with AI and checked by an editor.
  • Structured data: each item is a NewsArticle; /news/ is a CollectionPage. Add /news/ to the sitemap with changefreq: hourly (done in seo/sitemap.xml).
  • Log model, prompt version, tokens and cost per item. Expected cost is low at this volume; confirm current API pricing.

16.5 Front end

  • Filters: topic chips and a province select (All of Canada, Federal only, 13 provinces/territories). Reflect filters in the URL (/news/?topic=tax&province=ontario, noindex for filtered views).
  • Row: topic + province tags, headline, 2-line summary, deadline badge if set, source · date. No images.
  • The prototype shows grey placeholder rows. Replace them with live items; if the feed is empty, hide the homepage strip.
