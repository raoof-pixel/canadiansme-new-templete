# CanadianSME — project context

**Audience: Canadian small business owners.** The entire website is built for
them. Every design, content, and copy decision should serve a Canadian SMB
owner first — not investors, not enterprise, not a general business reader,
and not the publisher's own portfolio.

Practical implications:
- Copy should be concrete and Canadian: CRA deadlines, provincial and federal
  grant programs, BDC/EDC financing, Bank of Canada rate decisions, cross-border
  and tariff exposure, provincial regulation. Avoid generic global business copy.
- Canadian spelling (labour, centre, programme where appropriate).
- CanadianSME is the flagship. Sister titles (AmericanSMB, EuropeanSMB, Black
  Business, Startup Growth, She & Success, Indigenous-SME) are secondary on this
  site and should never outrank it visually.
- Reading level and tone: practical, plain, useful to an owner-operator who is
  short on time. Not corporate, not hype.
